Diet

An opportunistic carnivore. Its long snout and conical teeth were especially well suited to catching fish.

↕

Size

Up to 15 m long: roughly the length of a city bus. Its dorsal sail stood taller than an adult person.

⌖

Discovery

The first remains were found in 1912 in Egypt’s Bahariya Oasis by Richard Markgraf.

⌂

Visit

Chicago’s Field Museum displays “Sobek,” a full-scale reconstruction inspired by fossils from the Sahara.
